How It Works

Admission Process

Our admission process is simple and straightforward. Here's what to expect.

1

Submit Application

Fill the online form below or obtain a form at the school.

2

Entrance Assessment

Your child will take a brief age-appropriate assessment.

3

Parent Interview

A brief meeting with school management to discuss your child's needs.

4

Offer Letter

Successful applicants receive an offer of admission letter.

5

Pay Fees & Resume

Complete payment and your child is ready to begin!

What You'll Need

Admission Requirements

Please ensure you have the following documents ready when completing your application. Original documents and photocopies will be required.

  • Child's Birth Certificate

  • Recent Passport Photographs (x4)

  • Previous School Report Card (if applicable)

  • Immunisation / Medical Records

  • Parent / Guardian's Valid ID

  • Completed Application Form

School Fees

Fee Information

Our school fees are structured to be affordable while maintaining the highest quality of education for your child.

For detailed information about our current fee structure for each section (Nursery, Primary, and Junior Secondary), please contact us directly. Our school management will be happy to provide full fee details and discuss payment options.
